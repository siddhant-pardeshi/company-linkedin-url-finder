Privacy & local data

Company names and the optional disambiguation fields you provide are sent to your configured resolver, which forwards conservative search queries to a licensed search provider. Results and evidence are stored in this browser’s IndexedDB. No analytics, telemetry, LinkedIn credentials, or browsing history are collected.

The resolver reference implementation does not persist lookups; it keeps a short-lived in-memory idempotency cache and writes operational logs without company names or query text. Confirm your own deployment’s retention and the provider’s current terms before production use.
