When enabled, the extension sends only the person name, company, job title, and location to your chosen AI provider. Email and phone are never sent. The AI returns site-restricted search queries; the provider evidence and deterministic rules still decide the URL, and weak or competing results go to review.

Data handling

Privacy & local data

Company or person names and the optional disambiguation fields you provide are sent to your configured resolver, which forwards conservative search queries to a licensed search provider. If AI query assist is enabled, only the person name, company, job title, and location are sent to your chosen AI provider; email and phone are never sent. Results and evidence are stored in this browser’s IndexedDB. No analytics, telemetry, LinkedIn credentials, or browsing history are collected.

The resolver reference implementation does not persist lookups; it keeps a short-lived in-memory idempotency cache and writes operational logs without company names or query text. Confirm your own deployment’s retention and the provider’s current terms before production use.
